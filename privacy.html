The app is published by Michał Boryczko, an independent developer based in Poland (postal address available on request) (“we”, “us”), who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: watch.games.retro@gmail.com.

TLC Exam Prep is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission or PSI.

2. The short version

No account, no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifier. Your study progress stays on your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (you can turn them off in Settings) and, only if you choose to report a problem, the details of your report.

4. Who receives data

  • TelemetryDeck (usage statistics and reports): telemetrydeck.com/privacy
  • Apple (App Store, purchases, optional crash reports): apple.com/legal/privacy
  • GitHub (hosting the question file): GitHub Privacy Statement

These providers act under their own terms. Some are located in the United States; where required, they rely on recognized transfer mechanisms.

5. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device: until you delete it or the app.
  • Usage statistics: kept by TelemetryDeck in anonymous form for up to 24 months. Because they are anonymous, we generally cannot find or delete data about one person.
  • Reports: up to 24 months. E-mails you send us: until you ask us to delete them or 24 months after the conversation ends.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK, or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. Write to the address above. We will answer within one month (45 days in California). You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as defined by California law.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a driver license exam. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS. Question files are downloaded over HTTPS, and the app only accepts files encrypted with our key. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect very little data and keep no user accounts.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date, and announce material changes in a new app version.
