Privacy Policy

TLC Exam Prep (iOS app) · Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what data the app collects. In short: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your study progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

The app is published by Michał Boryczko (“we”, “us”), an independent developer. Contact: watch.games.retro@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your answers, review list, exam history, settings, exam date and the purchase status are stored only on your iPhone. We do not have access to them and they are not synced to any server. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also reset your progress in Settings.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

If statistics are on (Settings → Anonymous statistics, on by default), the app sends short events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics provider: for example that the app was opened, a practice session or exam was finished (with the score), a paywall was shown, a purchase was started or completed, and which app version, content version and interface/question language you use. The device language is also sent, to help us decide which languages to add.

Events are linked only to a random installation ID that the app generates and sends in hashed form. It is not your Apple ID, name, email or advertising identifier, and we cannot use it to identify you. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can turn statistics off at any time; the app then stops sending them.

Problem and question reports

If you use “Report an error in this question” or “Report a problem”, the app sends the question ID, the reason you chose and the optional note you typed, together with the app and content version. With statistics turned off, the report opens an email to us instead, and we receive whatever you write in that email, including your email address. Please do not put personal information in notes.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple (App Store). We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. We only learn, through anonymous events and Apple’s sales reports, that a purchase happened. Purchase status is verified on your device using transactions signed by Apple.

Content updates

At launch the app may download an updated question file from GitHub Pages (mboryczko.github.io), hosted by GitHub. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and standard technical data. We do not add any identifier to this request and do not receive these logs ourselves; see GitHub’s privacy statement. If the download fails or you are offline, the app keeps working with the content it already has.

Crash reports

If you allow Apple to share analytics and crash data with developers, Apple provides us anonymous crash reports through App Store Connect. We use no other crash-reporting service.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Service providers

TelemetryDeck (usage statistics and reports), Apple (App Store, purchases, optional crash reports) and GitHub (hosting of the content file). They process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention, your rights, children

Local data stays on your device until you delete it. Statistics are anonymous and kept by TelemetryDeck according to its retention settings. Because we cannot link statistics to a person, we generally cannot look up or delete data about you; if you sent us an email, you can ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA or UK) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data: contact us at the address above. If you are in the EU/EEA you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

The app is intended for adults preparing for a driver license exam and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in a new version of the app.
