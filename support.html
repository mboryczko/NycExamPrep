Support

TLC Exam Prep (the “app”), an iPhone app for practicing for the NYC TLC driver license exam. Last updated: October 16, 2026.

Contact us: watch.games.retro@gmail.com

We usually reply within a few business days. To help us, please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the question bank version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app). You can also write from the app: Settings → Help → Contact us.

TLC Exam Prep is an independent study app published by Michał Boryczko. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission or PSI. For official requirements, fees and exam scheduling, see nyc.gov/tlc.

Purchases

What is free and what is in the full version?

The free part includes a set of questions from every topic, one quick 20-question practice exam a day and one full 80-question practice exam. The full version is a single one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks all questions, unlimited full practice exams, NYC map questions and mistake review.

How do I restore my purchase on a new or reset iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy the full version, open the app and go to Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) → Full version → Restore purchase. The button is also on the unlock screen.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so we cannot issue refunds ourselves. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a promo code. Where do I enter it?

Open the unlock screen in the app and tap “Have a code? Redeem it”. You can also redeem it in the App Store app: tap your profile picture → Redeem Gift Card or Code.

Questions and content

I found a mistake in a question.

Thank you! After answering, tap “Report a problem with this question” below the explanation. For other problems, use Settings → Help → Report a problem. We aim to correct confirmed errors within 7 days, and corrections can reach the app without a new app version.

Are these the questions from the real exam?

No. The questions and explanations are written by us from public sources such as the TLC Rules (Title 35 RCNY) and New York State traffic law. They are not the questions from the real exam, and we cannot guarantee a pass. The sources are listed in the app under Settings → Licenses and sources.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language and text. You can choose the app language and the language of questions and explanations separately. Questions are available in English, Spanish and Bengali. Translations may contain errors; the English text is the reference.

Can I make the question text bigger?

Yes: Settings → Language and text → Question text size.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. After you download the app, you can study without an internet connection. Content updates are downloaded when you are online.

Progress and privacy

Is my progress synced between devices?

No. There is no account: your answers, reviews and exam history are stored only on your iPhone. Your purchase can be restored on any device with the same Apple ID, but progress starts from zero on a new device.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Practice by topic and tap Reset progress. This deletes all your answers, reviews and exam history on this phone and cannot be undone. It does not remove your purchase.

How do I turn off usage statistics?

Go to Settings → Privacy and switch off Anonymous usage statistics. The app has no ads and does not track you. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
